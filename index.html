Richard Mille's Endless Summer

(1) What was the first thing you paid attention to when interacting with the experience?

The visuals are warm and inviting, and the music is soothing. However, the webpage is unusable on a computer; it needs to be shrunk down to a mobile size to function. The experience of switching scenes by sliding the wheel at the bottom is quite enjoyable.

(2) Spend two minutes with the experience and create a list of each of your discrete actions.

  • Click "start" to begin playing.
  • Switch between scenes by sliding the wheel at the bottom.
  • In specific scenes, move the mouse up, down, left, and right to view scenes outside the camera's view (similar to a panoramic video).
  • You can also click the three-dot button to view special shots.
  • Click the title at the top to switch between different timelines.
  • You can quickly switch to the past or go back.

(3) What part of the experience did you spend the most time engaging with?

I spent the most time watching the special looping animations for each segment. Each segment was meticulously crafted. It was very enjoyable to watch and made me even more curious about the next animation.

(4) What was the most common action in your two minute interaction with the experience?

The most repetitive action is to slide the wheel at the bottom to switch scenes.

(5) What is your impression of the intended primary goal of the interactive experience?

Richard Mille wanted to draw viewers to his work through this relaxed, summery style, conveying the message that his watches are not only works of art but also companions in the user's life.

(6) How does the interactive experience communicate this primary goal?

The message is conveyed through visual effects. For example, showcasing vibrant scenes of life—tennis, surfing, diving, etc. This approach gives the watch a sense of life, suggesting that it's not just a luxury item, but a reflection of lifestyle.

(7) What is your impression of how the experience should be interacted with over time? (For how long and how many different times)

I don't think this is a website that's updated regularly. It's more like a magazine or journal. You can browse through it all in ten minutes on your first visit. It's more suitable for occasionally coming back to appreciate artwork.

(8) How does the interactive experience communicate how it should be interacted with over time?

This website displays a timeline from the start, letting users know it's a story with an end. If the author can create more diverse timelines and keep them updated, it will retain many users.

(9) What other media forms (digital or otherwise) does the experience reference?

This experience draws inspiration from various media formats. For example, it has a cinematic feel, with camera movement and scene transitions. It uses a pixelated art style, giving it a vintage feel. Combined with music and video modes, it creates an immersive experience.

(10) What does this reference/s communicate to you about how you should act when engaging with your research experience?

These references convey a relaxed and pleasant mood, drawing the viewer into the experience. It's like an invitation, not a task to be completed. It keeps the viewer engaged and curious about the next scene.

(11) What does this reference/s communicate to you about how you should feel when engaging with your research experience?

These references convey a relaxed and enjoyable feeling, emphasizing that users are enjoying the experience, rather than being forced to complete it.

(12) What is the most frustrating part of the interaction to you and what makes that part frustrating?

I think the most frustrating part is the interaction. When browsing on the computer web version, it needs to be converted into a mobile-like window to navigate. It would be better if it could be adapted to the computer web version. Secondly, I tried browsing on my mobile phone. When I wanted to watch panoramic videos, I had to swipe with my finger. It would be great if I could simply move my phone to watch panoramic videos, like in VR.

(13) What is the most satisfying part of the interaction to you and what makes that part satisfying?

What's most satisfying is the fantastic visuals and interactive features. The transitions between scenes are incredibly smooth, creating a highly immersive experience for the user. I think it's a masterpiece.
