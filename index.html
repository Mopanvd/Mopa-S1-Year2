Mopa

https://endless-summer.richardmille.com/

(1) What was the first thing you paid attention to when interacting with the experience?

The visuals are warm and inviting, and the music is soothing. However, the webpage is unusable on a computer; it needs to be shrunk down to a mobile size to function. The experience of switching scenes by sliding the wheel at the bottom is quite enjoyable.

(2) Spend two minutes with the experience and create a list of each of your discrete actions.

  • Click "start" to begin playing.
  • Switch between scenes by sliding the wheel at the bottom.
  • In specific scenes, move the mouse up, down, left, and right to view scenes outside the camera's view (similar to a panoramic video).
  • You can also click the three-dot button to view special shots.
  • Click the title at the top to switch between different timelines.
  • You can quickly switch to the past or go back.

(3) What did you like about the experience? What did you dislike?

I liked the warm and inviting visuals, soothing music, and the enjoyable scene-switching experience. However, I disliked that the webpage is unusable on a computer and requires shrinking down to a mobile size to function.

(4) What do you think the designer's intentions were in creating this experience?

I think the designer's intentions were to create an immersive and enjoyable experience that allows users to explore different scenes and timelines related to the Richard Mille brand. The use of warm visuals and soothing music suggests an intention to create a relaxing and engaging atmosphere for users.

(5) What would you change about the experience if you were the designer?

If I were the designer, I would optimize the webpage for desktop use to ensure that it functions properly on all devices. Additionally, I would consider adding more interactive elements or features to enhance user engagement and make the experience more dynamic.

(6) What other experiences does this remind you of?

This experience reminds me of other immersive web experiences and interactive storytelling platforms that allow users to explore different scenes and narratives, such as virtual tours, interactive documentaries, and multimedia storytelling websites.

(7) What other thoughts do you have about the experience?

Overall, I think the experience is well-designed and offers an engaging way for users to interact with the Richard Mille brand. However, improving the desktop functionality and adding more interactive features could further enhance the user experience and make it more accessible to a wider audience.

I would like to know more about the design process and the inspiration behind the visuals and music used in the experience.
